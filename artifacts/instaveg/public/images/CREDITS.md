# Image credits

All product and category photos in `public/images/products/` and `public/images/categories/`
are openly licensed photos from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), used under
the license listed for each image.

Changes made: every photo was resized and cropped to 800×800 and converted to WebP. Most product
photos also had their background removed and were placed on a plain off-white background with a
soft shadow and mild level correction; category images combine three of those product cutouts.
Images derived from CC BY-SA photos are shared under the same license.
